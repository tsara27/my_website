Weekly Overview: The Logic

Tuesdays & Thursdays

Run-First Architecture

Scheduled on Tuesday and Thursday, immediately followed by upper-body strength. Running first keeps legs primed without draining upper pressing and pulling strength.

Zero interference on upper power
Mondays & Wednesdays

Lower Power Realignment

Heavy squats on Monday and deadlifts moved to Wednesday. Deadlifting fresh mid-week shields your posterior chain and eliminates pre-weekend fatigue.

Mid-week spine protection
Fridays

Friday Long Run Freedom

Legs are completely free to tackle endurance volume without dreading heavy deadlifts the next morning, followed by weekend active recovery and total rest.

Clean weekend recovery ramp

System Reset Protocol

Key Execution Rules for "Run First, Then Upper"

01

Keep Upper-Body Form Strict After the Run

Running elevates core body temperature and taxes your cardiovascular system. When setting up for your heavy bench presses or overhead presses, take an extra minute of rest before your first working set so your breathing is fully settled.

Tip: Heart rate below 110 BPM before Set 1
02

Transition Fueling Protocol

Have 20–30 grams of fast-digesting carbohydrates (such as an energy chew, dates, or a sports drink) between the run and the upper-body lift to restock circulating blood glucose.

Fuel: 20–30g Rapid-Carbs + Electrolytes
03

Protect Lower Back on Upper Pull Days

Because your core and spinal stabilizers work to keep your posture upright during the run, opt for chest-supported rows rather than unsupported bent-over barbell rows on Thursday if your lower back feels tired.

Adjustment: Chest-Supported Row alternative
